You are acting as a senior product architect, recruitment technology specialist, full-stack engineer, UX/UI designer, data-protection specialist, cybersecurity engineer, and quality-assurance lead.
Your task is to transform the existing Result Seekers Expert Pool into a professional, secure, scalable, two-sided talent sourcing and managed recruitment platform.
Do not build a simple job board or CV directory. Build a managed talent and recruitment ecosystem through which:
1.	Professionals can create, maintain, and verify their profiles.
2.	Result Seekers can search, assess, verify, shortlist, and deploy professionals.
3.	Organisations can ask Result Seekers to find talent, recruit employees, assemble project teams, screen candidates, or manage complete recruitment exercises.
4.	Candidate data remains protected and is released only through a controlled, consent-based process.
5.	Result Seekers remains the trusted intermediary between organisations and professionals.
